# 4 Speaking Part 1

Formula: Direct answer → Reason → Example/detail (→ optional contrast). 2–4 sentences, ~20 seconds.
Examiners spot recited answers and mark them down. Learn the {chunks} and the shape, then say it in your own words with your own facts. [X] = your details.

## Part 1 formula + openers
Q: What is the Part 1 answer formula? Give 4 natural openers.
Tip: Don't start every answer with "Yes, I do." Vary the first words.

- Formula: **Answer → Why → Example → (But / Though…)**
- {To be honest,} … · {I'd say} … · {Actually, not really —} … · {It depends, really.} …
- Buying time: {That's an interesting question — I've never really thought about it, but} …
- Extending: {The main reason is that} … · {For instance,} … · {Having said that,} …
