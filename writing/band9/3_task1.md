# 3 Task 1 Academic

Formula: Intro (paraphrase) → Overview (2 main features, no numbers) → Body 1 → Body 2. No conclusion, no opinion.
Aim 170–190 words in 20 minutes. The overview is the single most important sentence for band 7+.

## Template: any chart
Q: Say the 4-paragraph Task 1 template.
Tip: Paraphrase: shows → illustrates / compares / gives information about · number of → how many · percentage → proportion.

**Intro:** {The [line graph] illustrates} [what] in [place] between [year] and [year].
**Overview:** {Overall, it is clear that} [biggest trend], while [second key feature].
**Body 1:** {Looking at the details,} [group A] {stood at} [x] in [year], {before rising steadily to} [y] by [year].
**Body 2:** {By contrast,} [group B] … {Meanwhile,} [group C] {remained relatively stable at around} [z].

## Template: process & map
Q: Say the intro + overview templates for a process diagram and for a map.
Tip: Process → present simple passive (is heated, is collected). Map → past simple passive / present perfect (was built, has been replaced).

**Process intro:** {The diagram illustrates the process by which} [X] {is produced}.
**Process overview:** {Overall, there are} [eight] {main stages in the process, beginning with} [A] {and ending with} [B].
**Process body:** {In the first stage,} … {Following this,} … Once [X] {has been} [dried], … {Finally,} …
**Map intro:** {The maps compare} [the town of X] in [year] and [year].
**Map overview:** {Overall, the area became far more} [urbanised], with [farmland] {being replaced by} [housing].
**Map body:** {To the north of} [X], [a park] {was built} … {The woodland in the east was cleared to make way for} …

## Sample: Line graph — internet access

Overall, {it is clear that} internet access increased in all three countries over the period, {with Country A remaining the most connected throughout}, while Country C {lagged considerably behind} the others.

In 2000, 40% of homes in Country A were online, {four times the figure for} Country B (10%). Access in Country A {rose steadily}, reaching almost every household (95%) by 2020. Country B saw {the most dramatic growth}: its figure climbed sharply to 50% in 2010 and continued upwards to 80% at the end of the period.

Country C, by contrast, started from a very low base of just 5%. Growth was slow in the first decade, with the proportion {only reaching 15% by 2010}. However, the rate of increase {accelerated in the second half of the period}, and by 2020 just over a third of households (35%) had access, which was still less than half the level of Country A.
